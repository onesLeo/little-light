# Improvement backlog

A review of the David & Goliath slice (2026-09-20): what to fix, what is done, and what to do next.
It came from playing the whole story in a window, reading the code, and measuring performance.
Status is one of **Done**, **Partly done** or **Open**.

Screenshots and measurements were taken at 1152x648 / 1280x720 on Windows with Godot 4.7.2 (Forward+).

## 3. Game feel

| # | Idea | Status | Why |
|---|------|--------|-----|
| 3.1 | Make Steady Hands a real (still fail-free) activity | **Done** | Hold Space / A / the BREATHE button to breathe in and a ring grows; let go to breathe out. Three slow breaths (about 4 s in, 4.5 s out, gradual turn-arounds, so it cannot be rushed) finish it. Wonder Light glows and David rises with the ring, and a soft air sound follows it. Cannot get stuck: it lets go by itself if held at full and breathes by itself if left alone. David also leans down toward his lamb for the whole activity instead of standing frozen (a "Crouch" shape key on his model, since he has no rig — see `generate_david_mentor_v4.py` and `steady_hands_minigame.gd`; a real rig for actual walking would be a much larger, separate task). See `docs/steady-hands.md`. **Not yet tried by a child or on a real tablet.** |
| 3.2 | Sound design | **Mostly done** | A synthesized music-box lullaby, wind, a stream that pans and swells as you near the water, birds, footsteps, a lamb that says "baa", and a wing rustle for butterflies (`soundscape.gd`, `tools/make_sounds.py`, `docs/sound-design.md`). Four buses with ducking under the voice, and Volume / Music / Sounds / Voices sliders in the pause menu. Verified by the smoke test and by measuring the files; the lullaby, the voice clarity and the stream level were checked by ear and adjusted; the lamb is a real CC0 sheep recording (pitched up, dried out), listened to and fine. Footsteps now differ on grass, the path and in the stream (`ground_surface.gd`), the stream ones now cut from a real recording of walking through shallow water instead of the old mud-pop stand-in — **listen check still to do.** |
| 3.3 | A world that reacts | **Done** | Grass and flowers lean away from the Wonder-Walker as they walk through (`meadow_sway.gdshader`, driven by `meadow_dressing.gd`). Fish get shy: within ~2.3 m they speed up, dive slightly and turn away (`stream_fish_swim.gd`). The collectible lamb breathes, then turns to face the player and hops when they come near, never leaving its spot (`lamb_life.gd`). Eight paper butterflies drift over the meadow and flutter off when approached (`butterflies.gd`). The lamb says "baa" when it notices you and butterflies rustle as they take off (3.2). David's companion sheep now breathes and glances at the Wonder-Walker too (`companion_sheep_life.gd`): it used to be welded into David's own single mesh at export time, so the Blender generator (`generate_david_mentor_v4.py`) now keeps it as its own joined object (`David_CompanionLamb` / `_Outline`, David v13), a separate node the life script can move without touching David's body. |
| 3.4 | Keep the player in the play area | **Done** | A soft boundary (`play_bounds.gd`): near the edge of a rounded-rectangle play area the walker meets a gentle push back that grows toward the edge, and cannot go past it; the first time it is felt, Wonder Light says a friendly line ("That's the edge of our little valley. Let's stay close!"). Also recovers a player who is placed far outside. Fixed a related bug: the stream pack's hidden `Bank_*` meshes still had collision, which made an invisible wall near x = -4 (`mesh_collision_baker.gd`). |
| 3.5 | Who is playing, saved progress and the Faith Journal | **Mostly done** | A "Who is playing?" screen (up to 4 children per tablet, each with a name and one of six paper pictures), and a Faith Journal per child holding the verses and charms they have earned, each read aloud when tapped. Saved on the tablet only (`profiles.cfg`). A press-and-hold "For grown-ups" area can empty a journal or remove a child. See `docs/faith-journal.md`. **Colour your charm** is in: tap a paint, tap a part of the charm; saved per child, shown in the journal and on the 3D charm in the ceremony (`colour_screen.gd`, `charm_art.gd`). **Also:** names that are rude words are turned away, the name form lifts above the on-screen keyboard, and colouring works with a gamepad or keyboard. **Open:** freehand drawing, stickers, and a check on a real tablet, especially the on-screen keyboard for typing a name. |
| 3.6 | Connect the Wonder Item hunt to David | **Done** | The prompt now says the stone, staff and little lamb are being found for David, checks each one off, and ends by leading the child's eye to David before the close-up. The meeting says the items were brought to him. The resolution also names Goliath and the sling, says David trusted God and defeated him, and keeps the action off-screen. The added clarification is read aloud in Wonder Light's existing Juno voice. |
| 3.7 | Spine: God is the source of courage | **Done** | Joshua 1:9 and “Don't. Be. Afraid.” play *before* Steady Hands, so the child has a promise to breathe. The breath intro, David's thank-you, the reflect line and the charm reason all name God-with-you rather than “feeling steady”. David still feels small afterwards. Recut in Juno/Bram (Seed Audio 1.0) so the voice matches the on-screen text (`docs/voice-over.md`). |
| 3.8 | Purpose and item flavour | **Done** | David names his job (keep the sheep safe) instead of “someone has to be brave”. Wonder Light names that job as why he will go. Reflect then gives the child a job too: stay close, and remember the words. The stone / staff / lamb pickup lines teach that job, and the resolution ties the hunted stone to what God used (“God can use even a small thing”) before naming Goliath off-screen. Matching Juno/Bram clips, including Easy-words takes. |
| 3.9 | God through David, for a parent who is buying | **Done** | The first line of the game names David and God. David then speaks 1 Samuel 17:37 in his own words (the lion and the bear). Joshua 1:9 stays the journal verse (public-domain WEB), with a spoken gloss that **Yahweh is God's name**. ## 4. Visual polish

| # | Idea | Status | Notes |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 4.1 | Sky and horizon | **Done** | Gradient sky, soft distance fog, four layers of paper-cut hills fading into haze, and slow-drifting paper clouds (`horizon_backdrop.gd`, `Environment` in `main.tscn`). The beige void is gone. |
| 4.2 | Ground edges | **Done** | The ground is now one painted 1024 px map (`paint_ground()` in `polish_valley_v7.py`) instead of one material per triangle, so the path, riverbank and cliff foot have smooth, wavy borders instead of sawtooth steps. |
| 4.3 | Trees on the cliff wall | **Done** | Ten of the 22 trees stood on ground the map paints as bare rock (steeper than about 36 degrees) or on the lip of the shelf, half over the drop. Each is now moved to the nearest grass: the foot of the back wall, the top of the shelf, or the ridge behind (`TREE_MOVES` in `polish_valley_v7.py`; the generator prints `TREE_ON_STEEP_GROUND` for any tree over 34 degrees). A second bug is fixed too: `stream_placement.gd` nudged trees away from the water sideways only, so on a bank they ended up buried up to 1.15 m or floating; it now sets them back on the ground where they land. The smoke test checks both. The wall itself is still bare rock. |
| 4.4 | Cliffs | **Mostly done** | The painted map gives the walls subtle strata, hairline cracks and a grassy rim. Flattened boulders of the valley's own stone (grey, mossy, slate, with the same inked outline) stick out of the cliff, half buried in it, with leafy tufts on top (`make_ledges` in `polish_valley_v7.py`; decoration only, no collision, clear of the waterfall) — widened from 3 to 4, now also covering a short stretch of the shelf's right-hand face, not just the front. The wall's own slab is no longer a single smooth ramp either: `height_at()` (`polish_valley_v6.py`) adds noise confined to the narrow cliff band itself (zero on the flat shelf top, zero on ordinary terrain), so the face and its top edge read as uneven, faceted rock instead of one flat plane between grid rows. Only where the wall is actually tall enough gets ledges (`make_ledges` skips the rest on its own); real overhang geometry in the wall would still need a finer terrain mesh than a heightfield allows. |
| 4.5 | Water | **Done** | New water shader (`stream_water.gdshader`, applied by `stream_water_fx.gd`): depth tint, ripples that flow downstream, a foam rim where water meets the bank, streaks pouring down the waterfall, and sparkle. |
| 4.6 | Meadow | **Done** | About 700 swaying grass tufts, 120 tiny flowers and 60 pebbles on open ground only (`meadow_dressing.gd`), as three MultiMeshes, so three draw calls. Different every run. |
| 4.7 | Dialogue box | **Done** | Paper-style panel (cream, ink border, rounded) with dark text, matching the pause menu. |
| 4.8 | Stone by the stream | **Done** | The 11 rocks of the stream pack were 20-sided tan lumps. `stream_rocks.gd` swaps each for one of the valley's grey, slate or mossy stones (limestone left out, since it is the tan they had), scaled to the size of the rock it replaces and turned at random, with the valley's outline. The colliders are baked from the new shapes. The pack itself has no generator in this repository, so the swap happens when the game starts. |
| 4.9 | Trees and bushes that look soft | **Done** | The leaves were sharp diamonds pointing straight out, so bushes looked like agave and olive crowns like spiky rings. Now every leaf is a rounded oval lying on a dome (or the cypress flame) like a paper shingle, with a thinner outline. All foliage is one vertex-coloured material, so a tree or bush is two draws instead of four to seven on Forward+ (`polish_valley_v7.py`, `foliage_colour.gd`). |
| 4.10 | Gameplay framing and readable dialogue | **Done** | The tabletop camera is modestly closer so the Wonder-Walker and items read better at 720p. The dialogue panel shrinks for short lines and grows for long ones instead of covering a fixed 200 px. Trees and shrubs directly between the camera and the child soften until the sightline clears (`foreground_fade.gd`). |

## 5. Technical and repository health

| # | Item | Status | Notes |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 5.1 | Performance | **Partly done** | Measured and thinned; see `docs/performance.md`. The characters were 62% of all triangles (266k for two figures about 100 px tall); they are now 101k with no visible difference, outline hulls no longer cast shadows, and phones and tablets draw the 3D picture at most 1600 px wide. Primitives per frame (shadows included) went from 793k to 361k. **Not yet run on a tablet**. Glow is now off on handhelds (little visible difference). Draw calls are still the likeliest cost: trees and bushes are now two draws each (Forward+ 523 to 351), but the Mobile renderer read 275 before and after. `tools/profile_frame.gd` repeats the measurement. |
| 5.2 | `.import` churn | **Done** | Cause found: 18 `.import` files had hand-typed identifiers (for example `uid://bq8streamfishalive01`, too long to be a valid Godot UID) and made-up cache hashes, so Godot replaced them on every import. They are now the files Godot generates; nothing referred to the old identifiers. After an import and a full test run, `git status` stays clean. Four missing `.gd.uid` files were committed too. |
| 5.3 | Line endings | **Done** | The repository already stored LF; the CRLF was only in Windows working trees (`core.autocrlf=true`). `.gitattributes` (`* text=auto eol=lf`, binaries marked) makes every checkout LF, so the warnings are gone. Renormalizing changed no file. |
| 5.4 | Repository size | **Done, with a limit** | `assets/` held 39 models but the game loads five. The other 34 versions (246 files with their `.import` / `.uid` files and textures, 49 MB) moved to `art/archive/models` with a `.gdignore`, so `assets/` is 33 MB (was 82 MB) and Godot no longer scans or imports them. Checked first: none is referenced, and the generators of the current models read no old model. **Note:** the files are still in the repository and its history, so a clone is not smaller; deleting them would not shrink history either without rewriting it. |
| 5.5 | Stale docs | **Done, by hand** | The README asset table was checked against `main.tscn` (five models, all current) and the docs that named moved files now point at the archive. Nothing checks the docs automatically. |
| 5.6 | Tests | **Partly done** | One headless smoke test (203 checks: story, input devices, touch, pause, the play area, the lamb, fish, butterflies, where trees and rocks stand, the performance budget, profiles and the Faith Journal, voice-over, the soundscape and Steady Hands) now runs on every pull request and push to `main` through GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/smoke-test.yml`, Godot 4.7.2 on Linux). It has run on GitHub and passes (about 35 seconds). Still open: nothing verifies how the game looks, so keep the screenshot helper in mind for visual checks. |

## Suggested order

1. Try touch and gamepad on real hardware (2.1). The voiceover and the lamb's "baa" have been listened to and are fine.
2. The trees, stream rocks and cliff wall/ledges (4.3, 4.4, 4.8, 4.9) are done; real overhang geometry in the wall would still need a finer terrain mesh than a heightfield. David's companion sheep moves on its own now too (3.3).
3. Try Steady Hands, the soundscape (including the new water-splash steps) and the Faith Journal with a child, on a real tablet, and tune them (3.1, 3.2, 3.5).
4. Run the game on a real tablet and profile it there (5.1, `docs/performance.md`). The repo housekeeping (5.2 to 5.6) is done.
5. Complete Chapter 2's real-device polish in 7.1, then lock Chapter 3's value and prototype in 7.2–7.3.
6. Build and test one journey at a time in the order listed in section 7. Refactor shared chapter code before duplicating it three more times.
7. Before a public validation demo, clear section 6. Before the paid store release, clear all of section 7 and follow [release-plan.md](release-plan.md).
